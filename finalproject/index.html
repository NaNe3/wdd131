Study Tip

When learning latin, it is necessary that you learn all of the conjugation and declension endings together. After which, you can compare across all of the different types of conjugations and declensions to see patterns and similarities. DO IT THIS WAY I BEG OF YOU!!!!! IT WILL BE OF THE GREATEST SUPPORT TO YOUR SUBSEQUENT STUDIES.
